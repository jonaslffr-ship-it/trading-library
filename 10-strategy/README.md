# 10 · Strategy & Applications 🟡–🔴

Turning the library's mechanics and methods into deployable applications — alpha generation and risk management. Where tracks A/B/D explain *how* markets move, C/F *how* you test and build, and E *why* they move, this track answers the fourth question: **how you actually deploy that knowledge to make money or protect capital.**

| `L3-model-to-trade.pdf` | 🔴 | Black-Scholes as a map; a delta-hedged option is a variance bet; can a free-data forecast beat the VIX? a pre-registered short-variance strategy |
